↳ how the arm works

This arm is a browser twin of the 5-DOF arm I built on an STM32. Every frame it runs inverse kinematics to turn where you point into five joint angles, keeping whichever elbow solution is closest to its current pose so it never flips. Each joint then follows its target through a critically damped controller with speed and acceleration limits, so it moves smoothly instead of snapping. Cursor jitter under 2px is ignored, and the wrist can't bend more than 100°.
